Nudge Privacy Policy

App: Nudge: AI Life Coach (Android, package tech.android.nudgeailifecoach)
Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Contact: gigrim@nudgeapp.tech

The short version
  • Nudge has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you enter is stored on your phone.
  • When you chat, your messages and a summary of what you track are sent to the AI service you choose (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI), using your own API key, so your character can reply.
  • The app blocker can see which app you open, but not what's on your screen. That stays on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is never sold.

1. Who we are

Nudge is an independent app made by one developer ("we", "us"). Nudge lets you create a character who texts you, helps you track habits, and blocks distracting apps. Questions about this policy go to gigrim@nudgeapp.tech.

2. Information stored on your device

Nudge stores the following only in the app's private storage on your phone. We (the developer) never receive it:

  • Your character: name, personality, age, sass and swearing settings, the name they call you, and any profile or widget photos you pick.
  • Chat: your messages, the character's replies, and facts the character saves about you ("memories").
  • What you log: goals, reminders, drinks and water, food and calories, workouts, treadmill sessions, body weight, study time, assignments, grades, spending and budget, and Pomodoro sessions.
  • App blocking settings: the apps you choose to block, focus sessions, schedules, daily limits, and an override code (stored only as a scrambled hash, never as the code itself).
  • Settings: colors, backgrounds, check-in hours, and the API keys you enter for Gemini, Claude or OpenAI.

4. Information shared with AI services you choose

Nudge's character is powered by an AI service that you pick in the app and connect with your own API key: Google Gemini (Google LLC), Claude (Anthropic PBC) or OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C.). When you send a chat message, or when Nudge writes a widget line, hourly check-in or calorie estimate, it sends that service:

  • your recent chat messages;
  • your character's name, personality and settings, and the name it calls you;
  • saved memories about you;
  • a summary of what you track: goals, reminders, drinks and water, food and calories, workouts, treadmill, body weight, steps, screen time per app, spending and budget, study time, assignments and grades, Pomodoro status, block schedules, and today's calendar events;
  • when you tap "Talk to [character]" on the lock screen, the name of the blocked app.

This is sent directly from your phone to that service over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It never passes through us. How the service handles it is governed by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API terms (note: Google may use content sent with free-tier keys to improve its products), Anthropic Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy. If you don't add an API key, nothing is sent to any AI service.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that receive your data, and we have no access to your chats or logs.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than running the app features you use.

6. Donations and emails

The Donate tab opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser. Any payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee under its own privacy policy; Nudge does not see your payment details. If you email us (for example to report a bug), we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply and fix problems.

7. Safety features

Nudge checks AI replies on your phone for harmful content (such as encouragement of self-harm) and, if a message you send sounds like a crisis, shows crisis resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US). These checks run on your device; nothing is reported to us or anyone else. Nudge's AI is not a medical, mental health or other professional.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app, clear the chat (Me → Clear chat), and delete memories (Me → What your character knows).
  • Uninstalling Nudge, or Settings → Apps → Nudge → Storage → Clear storage, deletes everything Nudge stored on your phone.
  • Data already sent to an AI service is kept according to that service's policy (linked in section 4). You can manage or delete it through your account with that service.

9. Security

Your data is kept in Nudge's private app storage, which other apps can't read under Android's app sandbox. Connections to AI services use HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, so protect your phone with a screen lock and keep your API keys private.

10. Children

Nudge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Characters in Nudge must be adults (18+). If you believe a child has used Nudge, contact us and we'll help remove any information.

11. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device and we don't hold copies, you can view, change and delete it directly in the app at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have additional rights; contact us and we'll help however we can.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
